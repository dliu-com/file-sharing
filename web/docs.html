How share.dliu.com is tested

如何检测 share.dliu.com

Machine-readable version: /docs/health.md机器可读版本：/docs/health.md

Every 15 minutes, status.dliu.com visits DL Share like a friend who hasn’t been sent a link: it never signs in and changes nothing. It opens the public pages, tries a link that doesn’t exist, starts (but never finishes) the owner sign-in, and asks the site’s own /health check whether the link store and the sign-in settings answer.

status.dliu.com 每 15 分钟访问一次 DL Share，就像一位还没收到链接的朋友：从不登录，也不改任何内容。它打开公开页面，试一个不存在的链接，发起（但不完成）站长登录，并通过网站自己的 /health 检查链接库和登录设置是否正常。

“Essential” functions take the whole site down when they stop; the optional ones only affect the owner.“关键”功能出问题时整个网站中断；非关键功能只影响站长。

What isn’t tested不检测的内容

  • Opening real shared files and folders: that needs a real share link, and the files come from OneDrive, which the tests never call.
  • Everything the owner does after signing in: creating, renaming, changing and deleting links.
  • Finishing the Microsoft sign-in, which needs the owner’s own account.
  • Removing a changed or deleted link’s files from the cache.
  • 打开真实的分享文件和文件夹：这需要真实的分享链接，而文件来自 OneDrive，检测从不访问 OneDrive。
  • 站长登录后的所有操作：创建、重命名、修改和删除链接。
  • 完成 Microsoft 登录，这需要站长自己的账户。
  • 链接修改或删除后，从缓存中清除其文件。
